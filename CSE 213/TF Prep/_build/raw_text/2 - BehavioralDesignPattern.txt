===== page 78 =====
Nafis Tahmid, Lecturer, CSE
Diﬀerence between Strategy and Template
78
➢
Template Method based on inheritance
-
Alter parts of an algorithm by extending those parts in subclasses. 
➢
Strategy is based on composition
-
Alter parts of the object’s behavior by supplying it with different strategies 
that correspond to that behavior. 
➢
Template Method works at the class level, so it’s static. 
➢
Strategy works on the object level, letting you switch behaviors at runtime.
