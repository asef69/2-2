===== page 5 =====
DIMENSIONS 1 & 2
Privacy & Data Ethics vs. Bias & Fairness
DIMENSION 01
Privacy & Data Ethics
Consent must be informed, explicit, and unburied. Gather 
only necessary data.
CASE STUDY: CAMBRIDGE ANALYTICA (2018)
Facebook data of 87M users harvested via quiz apps 
for political psychographics without explicit consent. 
Resulted in a $5B FTC fine.
DIMENSION 02
Algorithmic Bias & Fairness
Algorithms encode historical human biases unless 
proactively tested and mitigated.
CASE STUDY: AMAZON AI RECRUITING (2018)
Internal hiring tool trained on 10-year 
male-dominated resumes penalized 
candidates with the word "women's".
 Scrapped before deployment.

===== page 6 =====
DIMENSIONS 3 & 4
Accountability vs. Transparency & Explainability
DIMENSION 03
System Accountability
"The algorithm did it" is not an ethical or legal defense. 
Humans must own code outcomes.
CASE STUDY: VW DIESELGATE & BOEING MCAS
VW deliberately coded defeat software (11M 
cars affected). Boeing 737 MAX MCAS 
software concealed risks, causing 346 fatal 
crashes.
DIMENSION 04
Transparency & Explainability
Opaque "black-box" systems prevent users from contesting 
automated life decisions.
CASE STUDY: DUTCH CHILDCARE BENEFITS SCANDAL (2021)
An unexplainable risk algorithm falsely 
flagged thousands of dual-national families 
as fraud suspects, bankrupting victims and 
dissolving the government.

===== page 7 =====
DIMENSIONS 5 & 6
User Autonomy vs. Security as an Ethical Duty
DIMENSION 05
Autonomy vs. Dark Patterns
Software UX should support user intention, 
not manipulate cognitive vulnerabilities for 
revenue.
CASE STUDY: FTC V. AMAZON "ILIAD FLOW" (2023)
Amazon sued for designing a labyrinthine 
multi-step cancellation flow ("Iliad") to 
prevent users from unsubscribing from 
Prime while signup took one click.
DIMENSION 06
Security as an Ethical Duty
Negligent security patching and software race 
conditions inflict direct physical and financial 
harm.
CASE STUDY: THERAC-25 & EQUIFAX BREACH
Therac-25 software race conditions fatal-
dosed radiation patients. Equifax exposed 
147M records by ignoring a known Apache 
Struts patch for months.

===== page 8 =====
DIMENSIONS 7 & 8
Environmental Sustainability & IP / Generative AI
DIMENSION 07
Environmental Sustainability
Computation carries immense environmental costs in 
energy, water cooling, and carbon output.
CASE STUDY: LLM TRAINING CARBON FOOTPRINTS
Strubell et al. (2019) demonstrated 
training a single large model emits CO2 
equal to 5 cars over their lifetime, 
spurring the Green AI movement.
DIMENSION 08
IP & Generative AI Authorship
Generative AI blurs lines between data training reuse, 
licensing compliance, and original authorship.
CASE STUDY: NYT V. OPENAI & GITHUB COPILOT
Landmark lawsuits challenging 
unpermitted scraping of copyrighted 
news articles and open-source code for 
training commercial LLMs.

Ethics is a Design Constraint, Not an Afterthought.
Real-world failures trace back to treating ethics as an afterthought. Ask 
at design time: "Who could this harm, and how would we know?"
