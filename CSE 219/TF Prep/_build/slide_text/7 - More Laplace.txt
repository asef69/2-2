Transform-Domain View of an LTI System
• For an LTI system with impulse response h(t),
Y (s) = H(s)X(s)
where H(s) = L{h(t)} is the system function.
• Laplace transforms convert convolution in time into
multiplication in the s-domain.
• If the ROC of H(s) includes the jω-axis, then
H(jω)
is the frequency response.
• This is why poles, zeros, and the ROC are so valuable:
they reveal causality, stability, and the shape of the
response.
