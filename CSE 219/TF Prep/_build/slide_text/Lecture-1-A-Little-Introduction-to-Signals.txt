Even and Odd Signals

===== page 41 =====
Even signals are mirror-symmetric about the vertical axis
Property: x(−t) = x(t),
Example: x(t) = cos(t)

===== page 42 =====
Odd signals flip sign across the origin
Property: x(−t) = −x(t),
Example: x(t) = sin(t)
